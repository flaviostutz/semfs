# Installer Handshake Protocol

Before an installer leaves for a job, the operations lead should confirm the following:

- The mobile app build number matches the release approved for the region.
- The install QR pack includes the correct customer account identifier.
- The gateway serial number is already assigned in the provisioning dashboard.

## At The Door

1. Introduce HelioHome as the home management startup handling devices, app setup, and remote monitoring.
2. Confirm the customer knows which rooms will receive sensors or controllers.
3. Ask whether there are existing third-party electronics like Zigbee bulbs or Matter plugs.

## After Hardware Mounting

1. Pair the gateway before pairing edge devices.
2. Test lock, thermostat, and water alert automations from the same customer session.
3. Upload a photo of the installed hub and its power source.
