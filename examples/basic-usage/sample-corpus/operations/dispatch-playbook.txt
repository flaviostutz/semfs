HelioHome dispatch playbook

Use this when coordinating same-day visits for home management devices.

Dispatch order:
1. Confirm the customer is on site and that power is available at the hub location.
2. Check whether the job is thermostat-only, lock-only, or mixed hardware.
3. Prefer technicians certified for Zigbee pairing when the house includes more than five sensors.

If a hub is offline before arrival:
- Ask the customer to reboot the router first.
- If the status LED stays amber for more than five minutes, ship a backup gateway.
- Keep the original visit if the house also has a lock keypad issue.

Never dispatch after 18:30 unless the incident is tagged water leak or critical door access.
