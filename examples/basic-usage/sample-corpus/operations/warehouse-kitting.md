# Warehouse Kitting Instructions

Pack equipment by installation type.

## Climate Kit

- 1 thermostat controller
- 1 gateway hub
- 2 wall anchors
- 1 quick-start card

## Access Kit

- 1 smart lock body
- 1 keypad
- 1 battery tray
- 1 installer seal label

## Safety Kit

- 3 leak sensors
- 1 air quality monitor
- 1 spare USB-C power adapter

Attach a printed label with the customer code before the kit leaves the shelf.
