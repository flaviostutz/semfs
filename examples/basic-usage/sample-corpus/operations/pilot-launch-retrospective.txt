Pilot launch retrospective notes

The first 50-home rollout exposed three operational gaps:
1. Installers spent too long locating the correct breaker for powered thermostats.
2. Zigbee hubs near large televisions had more wireless interference than expected.
3. Customers needed a simpler explanation of what the gateway does.

Actions agreed:
- Add a pre-visit note asking residents to clear access to the breaker panel.
- Include a placement guideline that keeps the hub at least two meters away from major electronics.
- Create a one-page customer leave-behind with app login and support steps.
