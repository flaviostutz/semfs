# Incident Severity Matrix

This matrix is used by the startup operations team when electronics installed in homes stop responding.

## Severity 1

- Water leak sensor fails during an active leak.
- Smart lock cannot be unlocked remotely and the resident is locked out.

Response target: 15 minutes for triage, 2 hours for technician dispatch.

## Severity 2

- Gateway hub offline for more than 30 minutes in a home with more than three managed devices.
- Thermostat stops reporting temperature during business hours.

Response target: 1 hour for triage, same-day fix plan.

## Severity 3

- Camera privacy mode schedule is misconfigured.
- Air quality monitor is delayed by more than one reporting interval.

Response target: next business day.
