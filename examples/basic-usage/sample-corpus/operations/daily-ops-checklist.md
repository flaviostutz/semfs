# Daily Operations Checklist

HelioHome runs managed installations for thermostats, smart locks, leak sensors, and gateway hubs.

## Opening Shift

1. Confirm that the install queue is synced from the CRM by 08:00.
2. Verify that every field kit contains one gateway hub, one thermostat bridge, and two spare batteries.
3. Review overnight alerts for homes that went offline after firmware updates.

## Midday Review

1. Compare scheduled installs against device inventory reserved in the warehouse app.
2. Escalate any home with repeated packet loss above 12 percent.
3. Ask support to call the customer before dispatching a second technician.

## Closing Shift

1. Mark unused devices as sealed stock.
2. Tag returned electronics that show moisture damage.
3. Post the installer summary in the operations channel.
