Smart lock reset procedure

Run this procedure only after confirming the resident still has physical access to the door.

Reset steps:
1. Remove the inside battery cover.
2. Hold the program button for 10 seconds until the LED flashes red.
3. Reinsert the battery tray while keeping the button pressed.
4. Wait for the lock motor to cycle twice.
5. Re-pair the lock from the installer app.

If the lock fails to pair, verify that the gateway hub is online and within one room of the door.
