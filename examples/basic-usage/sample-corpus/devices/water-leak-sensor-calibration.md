# Water Leak Sensor Calibration

Leak sensors should be calibrated during installation and after every battery replacement.

## Calibration Flow

1. Clean both contact pins with a dry cloth.
2. Place the sensor on the included test pad.
3. In the service app, select Calibrate Sensor.
4. Wait for the baseline moisture reading to settle.
5. Trigger a simulated leak with two drops of water.

Expected result: alert should arrive in under 20 seconds.

If the reading is noisy, move the gateway away from microwave ovens or dense metal cabinets.
