# Air Quality Monitor Maintenance

The air quality monitor reports particulate matter, humidity, and carbon dioxide trends.

## Monthly Tasks

1. Wipe the intake vents.
2. Confirm the USB-C cable is not pinched behind furniture.
3. Review firmware status in the support console.

## Quarterly Tasks

1. Compare readings with a reference sensor.
2. Inspect for dust buildup caused by nearby electronics racks or media cabinets.
3. Replace the unit if carbon dioxide drift exceeds the approved tolerance.
