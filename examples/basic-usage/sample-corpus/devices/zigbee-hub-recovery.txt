Zigbee hub recovery guide

Use this when a gateway hub or Zigbee coordinator appears offline.

Recovery sequence:
1. Confirm the power adapter is the original 12V unit.
2. Check for an amber LED. Amber means the hub booted but did not rejoin the network.
3. Reboot the home router, then power-cycle the hub.
4. Wait five minutes for the mesh to reform.
5. Re-run network repair from the field app.

If more than three devices remain unreachable, move the hub away from large televisions, sound bars, or metal shelving.
If the LED stays amber after two power cycles, swap in the backup gateway and restore from cloud config.
